# CraftETA

Profession leveling 1-300 for WoW: Forever, in the ETA family look (LevelETA, GoldETA, LayerETA, TrackETA).

- Next to your profession window: your step in the route, the materials you have and need, Craft 1 / Craft all.
- How far it is: crafts, gold and time to your next rank and to 300. It tells you when to learn the next rank, and where.
- Shopping list that follows you while you craft: route materials from your skill now, minus bags, bank and mail.
- At the auction house (aux or Blizzard's): Scan prices for an estimate, then buy one item per click ("Buy 12 Copper Bar").
  The price is checked again right before each purchase, and above the estimate it asks first. Or send the list to Auctionator.
- Everything can be turned off in /ceta config.

Leveling routes from [WoW-Professions.com](https://www.wow-professions.com/forever), used with permission.
