# CraftETA

**Professions for WoW: Forever, in the ETA family look** (LevelETA, GoldETA, LayerETA, TrackETA).

![CraftETA](media/CraftETA-cf.png)

## Next to your profession window
- Your step in the 1-300 route, with the materials you have and need.
- **One Craft button that follows the guide**: it makes what the step still needs, and first any material for it you can make yourself.
- Skip a step, or go back to the one your skill is on.
- Crafts, gold and time to your next rank and to 300, and where to learn the next rank.
- It sits right of the profession window, or left when the right edge of your screen is busy.

## The main window (minimap button or /ceta)
- **Home**: your character in 3D, experience, gold, durability, bag space, time played, your professions and your next steps.
- **Alts**: a card for each character, and your whole account.
- **Professions**: the route for every profession as a bar from 1 to 300, with Craft now or Start farming.
- **Specializations**, **Crafters** (who in your guild can make it), **Gold** (craft profit and what is worth farming), **Codex** (recipes and where materials come from), **Camping** and **Cooldowns**.
- **Item tracker** and **farming tracker** (loot, value, gold per hour).

## Shopping and the auction house
- A shopping list from your route, minus bags, bank and mail.
- Works with **aux** or **Auctionator**, found by itself; buys one item per click with a price check, or sends the list to Auctionator.

Everything can be turned off in /ceta config.
